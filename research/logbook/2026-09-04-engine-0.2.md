## What was asked

A personal assessment of the whole project (science, implementation, feasibility), then: fix everything found, make the mathematics do the work rather than an LLM, and clean up whatever confuses other agents.

## What the assessment found

1. The engine was a typed-skeleton isomorphism matcher with lexical tie-breaking: fingerprints and affinity saw only nine roles and seven relation types; "semantics" was token Jaccard. Any two graphs with the same skeleton were "analogical".
2. Benchmark v0.1 was one 10-node template with eight label sets; cross-domain analogy was a relabelling; calibration and gate were structurally identical; the engine's own gate report said `fail` and R5 was accepted as a "qualified GO".
3. The regex extractor produced empty graphs on the project's own inputs; in the product the calling LLM did extraction.
4. Retrieval could not recover partial/granular/noisy positives (Recall@5 = 0 for those families) and made common motifs unretrievable by design.
5. Product: seven server entry points and two MCP tool vocabularies; import-time monkey patches; demo personas indistinguishable from people; OAuth issuer derived from `Host`; no audience check; unlimited guest creation; extractor labels could carry contact details.
