# Research Logbook — 2026-08-29

## Project framing

The project was reframed from "an experimental MCP research repo" to a direct product/research vision:

> **Shazam for human thought.**

The project goal is to represent aspects of human thought as structured signals and detect meaningful direct, approximate, analogical, and complementary resonance between independently evolving thoughts.

Research is a state of the project, not its definition.

## Human motivation

A recurring problem in human relationships and collaboration is that expectations are formed from weak proxies: vocabulary, titles, shared interests, biographies, social networks, or apparent agreement.

Resonance explores whether some of the underlying structure can be made observable before those expectations harden.

## Core technical direction

The current candidate pipeline is:

```text
human context
  -> Thought Graph
  -> sparse relational / multiscale fingerprints
  -> cheap candidate retrieval
  -> expensive structural alignment
  -> explainable resonance
```

The matching engine should not depend on an LLM making an opaque compatibility judgment. LLMs may remain useful for extraction, canonicalization, and explanation.

## Key analogy

Shazam became the organizing analogy not because thought is audio, but because Shazam converts a complex raw signal into sparse robust landmarks, retrieves candidates cheaply, and validates a coherent alignment across many local matches.

Working term:

> **Relational Constellation Fingerprinting**

## Scientific ground currently being investigated

- Structure-Mapping Theory / SME / MAC-FAC
- graph fingerprints / Weisfeiler-Lehman / graphlets
- approximate graph alignment
- Gromov-Wasserstein / Fused Gromov-Wasserstein
- multiscale / diffusion / graph coarsening methods
- external Knowledge DNA
- robust context-to-Thought-Graph extraction
- benchmark/falsification design

## Important architecture stance

Thought DNA will not be frozen before the matching methods and invariance requirements tell us what the representation must preserve.

The first question is therefore not "what node types feel right?" but:

> **What are the useful invariants of thought structure?**

## Execution decision

A public R0 research program was launched with independent model runs. The most critical algorithmic questions — fingerprint retrieval (B) and structural verification (C) — each receive two blind runs from different model families.

The repository will preserve missions, raw submissions, comparative reviews, decisions, rejected paths, and implementation consequences so the process is itself reproducible.

## Sprint constraint

The immediate architecture-to-MCP target is approximately 40–60 hours. This forces research to produce decisions and falsifiable experiments rather than broad literature surveys.
