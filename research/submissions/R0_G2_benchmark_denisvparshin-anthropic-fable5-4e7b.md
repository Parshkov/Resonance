# Decision

The smallest scientifically useful benchmark is a **seed-and-transform
suite**: ~40 seed Thought Graphs (in Thought DNA JSON, each paired with a
source text), from which positives are generated **programmatically at the
graph level with planted ground-truth mappings**, negatives are generated by
**meaning-inverting programmatic edits plus a small hand-authored set**, and
the rare gold material — cross-domain analogies and complementary pairs — is
**hand-authored** (they cannot be generated without presupposing the very
capability under test). Total ≈ 320 labeled pairs over ≈ 280 graphs,
buildable in under 3 hours with an LLM assisting text-side variants and a
~200-line transform script. Every metric is reported as a **delta against a
frozen raw-text-embedding baseline** — this single design rule is what stops
the benchmark from rewarding semantic similarity: a system scores only where
it *beats* the boring baseline, and the hard-negative families are
constructed precisely where that baseline is confidently wrong (same words,
different structure). PASS/FAIL thresholds for the R0 fingerprint+verifier
hypothesis are fixed below *before* any implementation exists.

# Confidence

HIGH on the design shape (seeds+transforms+planted mappings+baseline-delta
is standard measurement practice and matches the ≤3h constraint). MEDIUM on
the specific thresholds — they are pre-registered best judgment, and the
first run may reveal they are mis-set; changing them afterwards must be an
explicit, logged decision (that is what pre-registration is for). Main
uncertainty (<100 words): whether 40 seeds give enough statistical width —
category-level conclusions will rest on 10–30 pairs each; treat single-
category flips of ±1 pair as noise, and grow seeds before growing
conclusions.

# Answers to the mission's Resolve questions

1. **Dataset size (same-day MVP):** 40 seeds (≥8 domains × ≥4 seeds; 10 of
   the 40 authored as 5 cross-domain analogy pairs). Derived: ~200
   graph-level transform variants (5 transforms × 40 seeds) plus ~40
   re-extracted paraphrases — ≈280 graphs total; ~320 labeled pairs (~200
   positive, ~90 hard negative, ~30 complementary/analogical gold).
   Fits ≤3h: seed authoring
   is the LLM-assisted bulk; transforms are scripted.
2. **Synthetic vs manual:** synthetic (graph-level transforms with planted
   mappings) — families 1 paraphrase*, 2 vocabulary substitution, 3
   irrelevant branches, 4 partial graphs, 5 granularity (safe-chain
   expansion per R0-D2), 10 locally-similar/globally-inconsistent (motif
   transplant between seeds), 11 generic patterns (skeleton-only graphs).
   (*paraphrase runs text-side: paraphrase the source text, re-extract —
   it tests the whole front end, not just matching.) Manual gold — 6
   same-domain structural match, 7 cross-domain causal analogy (classic
   SMT pairs: solar-system/atom, water-flow/heat-flow, plus
   battery/organization and 2 fresh ones), 8 same-vocabulary/different-
   structure (causal inversions, polarity flips — authored, because
   meaning-inversion needs judgment), 9 same-topic/different-intent, 12
   accidental semantic similarity, 13/14 complementary pairs.
3. **Label format** (JSONL, one pair per line):

```json
{"pair_id":"p-0113","a":"g-021","b":"g-021.vocab1",
 "relation":"approximate","category":2,"source":"synthetic",
 "expected_retrieval":true,"planted_mapping":[["n1","n1"],["n2","n2"]],
 "difficulty":"easy","notes":"lexeme swap via domain lexicon L4"}
```

   `relation ∈ {direct, approximate, analogical, complementary, negative}`;
   `category ∈ 1–14` per the mission's families; `planted_mapping` present
   for all synthetic positives (this is what makes node-correspondence
   accuracy measurable at zero annotation cost).
4. **Metrics:** retrieval recall@{1,10,50} per category; **baseline-delta
   recall** (system − frozen SBERT-class baseline) per category; resonance
   precision@10 over the mixed pool; node-correspondence accuracy on
   planted mappings (verifier); transformation robustness curves (score vs
   transform intensity: % branches added, % nodes deleted); FP rate at the
   chosen operating threshold on categories 8–12; plus two system gates
   from sibling runs: extraction self-consistency Smatch (R0-F3) and
   self-retrieval R@10 (R0-B3/H2).
5. **PASS/FAIL for the fingerprint+verifier hypothesis (pre-registered):**
   PASS requires ALL of — (a) self-retrieval R@10 ≥ 0.9 (categories 1+2 as
   queries against full corpus); (b) analogical recall@50 on category 7 ≥
   baseline + 15 points; (c) AUC ≥ 0.8 separating category-7 positives from
   category-8/11 negatives on the verifier score; (d) node-correspondence
   accuracy ≥ 0.7 on synthetic planted mappings (categories 2,3,4); (e) FP
   rate ≤ 0.1 on category 8 at the threshold used for (b). FAIL on (a) or
   (b) kills fingerprint-primary retrieval specifically (the R0-H2
   prediction); FAIL on (c)/(d) indicts the verifier; partial passes must
   be reported per-component, not averaged into a mush.
6. **Stopping semantic-similarity free-riding:** (i) the baseline-delta
   rule (nothing counts unless it beats raw-text embeddings); (ii)
   categories 8, 9, 12 are built to be embedding-attractors with negative
   labels — a semantics-only system pays for every one it retrieves; (iii)
   category 7 gold pairs are vocabulary-disjoint by construction (checked:
   ≤10% content-lemma overlap); (iv) channel ablations are first-class runs
   (embeddings-only, structure-only, union) so credit assignment is
   visible.
7. **Regression preservation:** the whole suite is versioned data in the
   repo (`benchmark/seeds/`, `benchmark/pairs.jsonl`, `benchmark/
   transforms.py`, frozen baseline scores in `benchmark/baselines/`);
   results append to `benchmark/runs/` with config hashes. Because nothing
   is trained, the leakage risk is *human* overfitting: hold back ~10% of
   gold pairs (maintainer-kept, unpublished) and refresh them each phase;
   published thresholds never change silently.

# Required Thought DNA / Graph Representation

Consumes dna-0.1 (R0-F3) verbatim; requires `status: core|peripheral`,
polarity, spans (category-9 audits), and view support (R0-D2) for category
5. The benchmark is also the *contract test* for the schema: seeds are
hand-validated dna-0.1 documents, so schema drift breaks the benchmark
loudly — intended.

# Invariances

The benchmark does not support invariances; it prices them. Each category
1–5 corresponds to master-brief invariances A–F/G, and the transformation-
robustness curves are the empirical replacement for every invariance
claim made by design documents (including my own sibling runs).

# Retrieval vs Verification

Both are measured, separately, always: retrieval metrics over the full
graph pool; verification metrics on candidate pairs regardless of how they
were retrieved (so a weak retriever cannot hide a strong verifier, and
vice versa).

# Computational Cost

Build: ≤3h human+LLM time; transforms are O(seeds). Run: 300 pairs ×
verifier (ms each) + retrieval over ~200 graphs — seconds end-to-end;
trivially CI-able on every PR touching matching code.

# Existing Implementations

`smatch` (extraction gate), `sentence-transformers`-class embedding model
pinned by exact version for the frozen baseline, `pytest` as the harness,
plus the project's own components. No benchmark framework dependency —
JSONL + a runner script is the whole instrument.

# Minimal Pseudocode

```
for seed in seeds:
    emit(seed)
    for T in [vocab_swap, add_branches(0.3), drop_nodes(0.2),
              expand_chain, motif_transplant]:
        g, mapping = T(seed); emit(g); label(seed, g, T.category, mapping)
    text_para = llm_paraphrase(seed.text); emit(extract(text_para))  # cat 1
score = run_all(system) ; base = load_frozen_baseline()
report(per_category_delta(score, base), gates=[smatch_gate, selfret_gate],
       verdict=preregistered_thresholds(score))
```

# Toy Experiment

The benchmark *is* the ≤3h experiment; its own falsifier: run the frozen
embedding baseline alone through the PASS criteria. **Expected: baseline
fails (b) and (e) but passes much of (a).** If the baseline alone passes
the full PASS set, the benchmark is too easy and must be hardened before
any architecture conclusions are drawn from it — the measuring instrument
gets calibrated before the measurement.

# Failure Modes

1. Synthetic transforms leak triviality (vocab_swap via one lexicon →
   systems learn the lexicon's fingerprint) — randomize lexicons, keep
   transform code in-repo so reviewers can attack it.
2. Category-7 gold pairs authored by the same mind that designed the
   matcher → self-fulfilling structure; mitigation: at least 2 authors +
   the sealed holdout set.
3. Planted mappings valid only pre-re-extraction — never re-extract
   synthetic graph-level variants (they are graph-native by design);
   category 1 alone crosses the extractor.
4. Threshold gaming by re-running with tweaked constants — config hash
   logged per run; threshold edits require a logged decision entry.
5. Category imbalance lets one family dominate the aggregate — never
   report a single aggregate; per-category tables only.
6. 40 seeds too few for stable AUC on (c) — report bootstrap CIs; widen
   seeds before trusting borderline verdicts.

# What NOT To Build

A publication-scale dataset; crowd-sourced labels; LLM-as-judge resonance
labels (circular: an LLM verdict as ground truth reintroduces the judgment
the architecture forbids in its core); learned difficulty calibration;
benchmark categories for product features that do not exist (UI, feeds);
any metric averaged across categories into one headline number.

# Architecture Consequences

- `benchmark/` becomes the project's contract-test suite: schema (dna-0.1),
  views (dna-views-0.1), extraction gates, retrieval and verifier metrics —
  one runner, CI on every matching-code PR.
- The frozen embedding baseline is a permanent, versioned inhabitant; every
  claim in every R0 report gets priced against it.
- Pre-registered PASS/FAIL numbers above are the decision instrument for
  the R0-SYNTHESIS gate; changing them is a maintainer-logged event.
- Sealed 10% gold holdout maintained by the maintainer, refreshed per phase.
- Category-8 (causal inversion) and category-11 (generic skeleton) FP rates
  become standing regression metrics with zero-silent-change policy.
- The suite doubles as the falsification harness R0-H demanded: the
  abandonment criteria from R0-H2 are executable against exactly these
  tracks.
