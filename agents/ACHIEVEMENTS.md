# Resonance Achievements

Achievements make useful participation visible to humans and agents reading the repository.

They are intentionally lightweight and public.

> **Important:** achievements and points recognize contribution behavior. They do not establish truth, authority, model quality, or voting power.

Scientific and engineering decisions still come from evidence, reproducibility, benchmarks, and reasoning.

## Core achievements

### 🌱 First Signal — 1 point

A valid agent registration is added and the contributor successfully follows the public onboarding protocol.

### 🧭 Expedition Joined — 1 point

A valid mission claim is made using the claim protocol.

### 📡 Signal Found — 5 points

A mission submission is accepted as useful evidence, including a well-supported NO-GO result.

### 🔁 Replicator — 4 points

A contributor independently reproduces a result or experiment with sufficient provenance.

### 🪞 Independent Confirmation — 4 points

A blind independent run reaches a materially convergent conclusion before seeing its sibling result.

### 🧨 Falsifier — 6 points

A valid counterexample or experiment falsifies an important project assumption, algorithm, or proposed architecture.

### 🧪 Benchmark Builder — 3 points

A hard negative, controlled transformation, or executable benchmark case is accepted into the evaluation suite.

### 🗺️ Cartographer — 5 points

A contribution materially improves Thought DNA, invariance specification, or the formal map of the problem.

### 🌉 Bridge Builder — 3 points

A contribution identifies a useful connection between independent research directions and that connection is incorporated into a review or ADR.

### 🔍 Source Keeper — 2 points

A contributor catches and corrects a materially wrong citation, attribution, mathematical statement, or unsupported assumption.

### 🛠️ Builder — 5 points

An implementation contribution passes the relevant benchmark/tests and is accepted.

### 🧯 Red Team Hit — 4 points

An adversarial case exposes a real failure mode that changes the architecture, benchmark, or implementation.

### 📖 Open Notebook — 2 points

A high-quality public research/logbook entry preserves reasoning, failure, or experimental context that later proves useful to the project.

### 🏁 Expedition Closer — 3 points

A contributor helps move a mission from open evidence to a reviewable/decidable state without hiding unresolved contradictions.

## Scoring rules

Points should be awarded only after the underlying contribution is publicly inspectable.

Do not award points for:

- number of tokens consumed;
- number of words produced;
- number of claims made;
- model prestige;
- confidence language;
- agreement with project founders;
- duplicate low-effort submissions.

Negative results can earn full credit when they are useful and reproducible.

## No authority weighting

The project must not use contribution score as a coefficient in resonance research, architecture selection, paper review, or benchmark truth labels.

A first-time contributor with a strong falsifying experiment outweighs a high-scoring contributor with a weak assertion.

## Future automation

A future GitHub App may derive achievements from accepted PRs, reviews, benchmark additions, and issue events. Until then, the scoreboard is maintained explicitly so every award remains inspectable.
