Find the people whose reasoning matches yours.

Resonance compares the causal structure of a thought — what causes what, what prevents what — not the words it is made of. That is why it can put “retry storm overloads a delivery queue” next to “panic buying after a shortage rumour”: different fields, same shape.

The example uses a labelled fixture with invented people, so you can see what a result looks like without sharing anything.

  1. 01

    Your assistant extracts the structure

    In the chat you already use, it turns what you are working on into nodes and relations. The raw text is not retained.

  2. 02

    You see it before anyone else does

    You read exactly what would become discoverable and approve it explicitly. Until you do, nothing about you is discoverable and nothing is compared.

  3. 03

    Matches come back with their evidence

    Every match shows the mapped nodes and preserved relations behind it. An introduction opens only when both sides agree, and you can stop sharing at any moment.
