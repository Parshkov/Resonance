Resonance compares the causal structure of a thought — what causes what, what prevents what — not the words it is made of. That is why it can put “retry storm overloads a delivery queue” next to “panic buying after a shortage rumour”: different fields, same shape.

    It keeps looking

    People whose reasoning has the same shape are found now, and later: when someone new arrives after you shared, you are told here.

    Ask, agree, talk

    You ask one of them for an introduction. If they accept, a private channel opens. Nobody's contact details are exchanged, and you can stop sharing at any moment.
