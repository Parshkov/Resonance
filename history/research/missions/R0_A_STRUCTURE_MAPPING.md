## Decision question

What mathematical and representational properties must a Thought Graph preserve if Resonance is intended to detect human-recognizable structural analogy rather than merely topical similarity?

- MAC/FAC

- causal analogy

4. Do relations themselves need to become nodes/objects, or is another representation better?

6. What parts of classical SME should we explicitly not reproduce?
7. How should relational analogy be scored separately from semantic similarity?
8. Which Thought DNA fields become mandatory because of this decision?
