# Resonance Vision

Resonance is an attempt to make a hidden human phenomenon measurable: the degree to which independently evolving thoughts, expectations, problems, and lines of reasoning actually align.

People usually discover this late, through conversation, collaboration, conflict, friendship, or chance. We often form expectations from weak proxies: shared vocabulary, profession, biography, social proximity, or stated interests.

Resonance asks whether some of that alignment can be observed earlier.

The long-term direction is simple to state:

```text
thought
  -> structured signal
  -> resonance
  -> person
```

A person's raw context is not the thing we ultimately want to search. We want a representation of the structure within it: goals, constraints, hypotheses, mechanisms, causal relations, knowledge requirements, missing branches, and the relationships among them.

If that structure can be converted into a stable enough signal, then two people do not need to know one another's names in advance for their ideas to become discoverable to one another.

## What we want to make possible

A system should eventually be able to say things like:

- "Another person is independently reasoning about the same hidden structure using different language."
- "These two thoughts use similar terminology but their causal structures do not align."
- "This person's reasoning reaches the point where yours currently stops."
- "Your apparent agreement may be superficial; your expectations about the system diverge here."
- "These two different domains instantiate a useful structural analogy."

Resonance should provide evidence, not social judgment. It should not decide whom someone should trust, hire, love, follow, or believe. It should make relevant structure more visible so people can make those choices with a better signal.

## Why mathematics matters

We do not want the identity of Resonance to be "an LLM that reads two biographies and has an opinion."

LLMs can help translate human language into a structured representation and translate mathematical results back into human language. The comparison itself should be grounded in inspectable representations, topology, graph structure, fingerprints, alignment, and measurable invariances wherever practical.

In principle, a correctly formed Thought Graph should be comparable even if no LLM is present in the matching loop.

## Why the work is public

The project is also a live experiment in human-agent collaboration. The questions, missions, independent agent reports, disagreements, rejected methods, benchmarks, and decisions are part of the artifact.

We want someone arriving later to be able to see not only **what** Resonance became, but **why**.
